## 12. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

1. **Sep 25, 2026 is sacred** — CP curriculum complete. Nothing moves it.

7. **No videos.** Text resources only (existing rule).

9. **Sunday review is non-negotiable** — 10 minutes, keeps all 3 tracks honest.
10. **If you fall behind 3+ days: cut GSoC skim books first, never contests, never notes.**
