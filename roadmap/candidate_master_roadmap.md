## 6. RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

1. **No videos.** Text resources only: the 3 books, CP-Algorithms, USACO Guide, Striver sheet articles.
2. **Read before solve.** Every new topic: read the CP-Algorithms article and book chapter first, then solve.
3. **45-minute rule (CP-31):** struggle 30-45 min, then read the editorial, understand it, and code it yourself. Never skip the editorial.
4. **Never skip upsolving.** A contest without upsolving is 50% wasted.
5. **Notes daily (30 min).** One page: new patterns, mistakes, tricks. Becomes the Sep 24 review doc.
6. **Template updates:** every new advanced technique goes into `templates/`.
7. **Streaks protect everything:** minimum day = 21 pages + 3 problems. Never zero.
8. **Sleep 7+ hrs.** A tired brain loses every contest.
9. **Priority when time is short:** contests > practice > reading.
10. **Track it:** update the progress tables in Section 7 every Sunday.
